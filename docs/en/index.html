SUDOMAKE PartitionRead, copy and mount Mac and Linux disks on Windows

Windows cannot open Mac (APFS, HFS+) or Linux (ext4, LVM) disks. SUDOMAKE Partition can: it shows the partitions as tiles, tells you which system is on each one, and lets you copy the files or mount the disk as a drive letter. Free, open source and strictly read-only: nothing on the original disk is changed.

What it does

For the internal disk taken out of a MacBook, iMac or a PC running Ubuntu, Debian, Kali or Fedora, for external disks and USB sticks formatted on Mac or Linux, and for disk images.

🍎

Mac disks

APFS (macOS 10.13 to the latest, all volumes, compressed files) and HFS+/HFSX (Mac OS Extended, Time Machine).

🐧

Linux disks

ext4, ext3 and ext2, also inside LVM volumes. Ubuntu, Debian, Kali, Mint, Fedora, RHEL…

💽

Disk images

DMG (zlib, bzip2, ADC, LZFSE, LZMA), .img, .raw and .dd, with GPT, MBR and Apple Partition Map tables.

🔎

Detects the system

Every partition shows what it holds: "Ubuntu 22.04", "Kali Linux", "macOS 12.5", "Windows", "EFI", "swap", "Time Machine"…

🔤

Mount as a drive

The volume becomes a drive letter (Z:), read-only, visible in Explorer and in any program. Several disks at the same time.

📦

Copy everything safely

Copy folders or the entire disk with progress, resume and an error log. Before copying a whole disk it checks the free space at the destination.

🔒

Read-only

It never writes to, repairs or changes the source disk. No password needed for ordinary disks (only encrypted ones are excluded).

🌍

4 languages and themes

Portuguese (Angola), English, French, Spanish. Light, dark or system theme. Disks plugged in or removed appear on their own.

How to use

Install

Download the installer and follow the wizard. If the Dokan driver (only needed to mount drives) is missing, the installer takes care of it.

Plug in the disk and open

Connect the disk via USB or an adapter and open the program. Accept the Administrator prompt: Windows requires it to read physical disks.

Click the partition

Personal files live in /Users/name (Mac, "Macintosh HD - Data" volume) or /home/name (Linux).

Open, copy or mount

Open in the program to browse and copy; Mount as drive to use the disk in any program; Copy entire disk to move everything to another disk.

Frequently asked questions

The most common questions from people who need to read a Mac or Linux disk on a Windows PC.

Windows says the disk needs to be formatted. Will I lose my data?

Do not format. Windows does not recognise APFS, HFS+ or ext4, so it suggests formatting. Close the prompt, open SUDOMAKE Partition and the disk appears with all its partitions and files.

How do I read a MacBook or iMac disk on Windows?

Connect the disk (USB/SATA or NVMe adapter, or the external drive), open the program, click the "Macintosh HD - Data" volume and copy the /Users/<name> folder. You can also mount the volume as a drive letter (Z:) and open the files in any program.

How do I open an ext4 partition from Ubuntu, Debian or Kali on Windows?

Open the program, click the partition labelled with the system name ("Ubuntu 22.04", "Kali GNU/Linux"…) and copy /home/<name>, or mount it as a drive. Partitions inside LVM show up as separate volumes.

Is it safe? Does it change anything on the disk?

No. The program is read-only: it never writes, repairs or mounts in write mode. The disk stays exactly as it was and can go back to the Mac or Linux machine.

Do I need the Mac or Linux password?

Not for ordinary disks: the login password only protects the session, the files are stored unencrypted. Only deliberately encrypted disks (FileVault, LUKS, eCryptfs) cannot be opened.

How much does it cost? Is it an alternative to Paragon, MacDrive or Linux Reader?

It is free and open source (MIT license), with no limits and no paid edition. It reads Mac and Linux in the same program, tells you which system is on each partition and installs no drivers for reading or copying. If it helps, a donation of any amount is welcome.

    Requirements and notes

    • 64-bit Windows 10 or 11. Nothing else needs to be installed.
    • The installer (recommended) installs for your user or for everyone, creates shortcuts and an uninstaller, and downloads the Dokan 2 driver if missing.
    • The portable version (zip) needs no installation: unzip and run SudomakePartition.exe.
    • Reading a physical disk requires Administrator rights; the program asks for them when it opens.
    • It does not open encrypted disks (FileVault, LUKS, eCryptfs) or T2 / Apple Silicon Macs.
